The debate between dividend investing and growth investing is central to portfolio strategy, particularly for novice investors. Dividend stocks provide regular income through profit sharing, while growth stocks reinvest profits for capital appreciation. Understanding the trade-offs—income stability vs. long-term price upside—helps investors align choices with their financial goals, risk tolerance, and time horizon.
