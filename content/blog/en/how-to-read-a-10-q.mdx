The 10-Q is a quarterly report that U.S. public companies file with the SEC, providing a detailed update on their business, risks, and financial results for the quarter. It is a critical document for investors and analysts to track a company's performance and identify emerging issues between annual reports.

## What the data shows

- Most U.S. public companies must file a 10-K each year and a 10-Q after each of the first three fiscal quarters (Investor.gov).
- SEC rules require 10-Ks to follow a set order of topics (Investor.gov).
- The 10-Q is required to be filed after the first, second, and third fiscal quarters (Investor.gov).
