## FAQ

**What is the best brokerage account for beginners in 2026?**

Fidelity is the stronger choice for beginners. The platform requires no minimum deposit and offers zero expense ratio index funds. Its interface simplifies portfolio setup without overwhelming new users with advanced trading tools. The automated cash sweep feature also earns interest on uninvested cash without requiring manual money market trades.

**Which online broker has the lowest margin rates in 2026?**

Interactive Brokers consistently offers the lowest margin rates in 2026. Rates start below 5 percent for large balances and scale down with account size. Fidelity charges higher standard margin rates that can exceed 8 percent for smaller balances. Active traders using margin should calculate the interest differential based on their specific borrowing needs.

**Do any online brokers still charge commissions for stock trades?**

Major brokers on NerdWallet's 2026 list do not charge commissions for stock and ETF trades. Fidelity and Interactive Brokers both offer zero-commission trading on US-listed equities. However, Interactive Brokers charges tiered or fixed pricing for options trades and non-US exchange access, which adds costs for active derivatives traders.

**How do I choose a brokerage account for long-term investing?**

Focus on expense ratios, cash sweep rates, and account fees. Fidelity wins here with zero expense ratio mutual funds and no account minimums. Interactive Brokers charges an inactivity fee for smaller accounts. Long-term investors should also verify that the broker offers automatic dividend reinvestment and fractional share purchasing without limitations.

**What is the best online broker for direct international stock trading?**

Interactive Brokers is the clear winner for international trading. The platform provides direct market access to over 150 global exchanges across multiple currencies. Fidelity only offers international exposure through US-listed ADRs or international ETFs. Investors needing direct foreign market access must choose Interactive Brokers to avoid ADR limitations and currency conversion workarounds.

**Are there differences in customer support between Fidelity and Interactive Brokers?**

Fidelity maintains 24-hour phone support with shorter wait times and local branch access in major US cities. Interactive Brokers offers 24-hour phone support but frequently experiences longer wait times during peak market hours. The Motley Fool's August 2026 update specifically highlighted customer service quality as a differentiating factor for retail-focused brokers.
