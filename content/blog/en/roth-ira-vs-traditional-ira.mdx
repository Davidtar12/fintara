Roth IRA vs Traditional IRA comparison is central to retirement planning, focusing on differences in tax treatment: Roth IRAs offer tax-free growth and tax-free withdrawals in retirement (contributions made with after-tax dollars), while Traditional IRAs provide tax-deferred growth with pre-tax contributions (taxed upon withdrawal). Key distinctions include eligibility income limits, required minimum distributions (Roth has none, Traditional requires RMDs), and withdrawal rules. The choice often depends on current vs. expected future tax bracket and income level.

## What the data shows

- Roth IRA contributions are made with after-tax dollars and are not tax-deductible.
- Roth IRA potential earnings grow tax-free and qualified withdrawals (including earnings) are tax-free after age 59½ and meeting 5-year rule.
- Traditional IRA contributions may be tax-deductible depending on income and coverage by a workplace plan; earnings grow tax-deferred.
