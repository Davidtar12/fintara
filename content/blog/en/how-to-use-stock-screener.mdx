Stock screeners are tools that allow investors to filter stocks based on predefined criteria such as market capitalization, price-to-earnings ratio, dividend yield, sector, and other fundamental or technical metrics. They are essential for efficiently narrowing down thousands of stocks to a shortlist that matches specific investment strategies, saving time and improving research quality.

## What the data shows

- Stock screeners can filter by market cap, P/E ratio, dividend yield, sector, and 52-week price range (general knowledge).
- Many online brokers offer free screener tools as part of their platform (general knowledge).
- Screeners typically provide a list of tickers with key data points like price, volume, earnings date (general knowledge).
